The Opportunity Nobody's Talking About

Here's something most people don't realize about Utah tap water. It passes federal standards. That sounds reassuring until you learn those standards haven't been updated in almost 20 years. The EWG found 89 contaminants in Utah water systems. Arsenic at 135 times above health guidelines. Bromochloroacetic acid at 168 times.

Now think about how many businesses along the Wasatch Front are still paying for 5-gallon bottle delivery — stacking jugs in the corner, watching invoices creep up, and putting up with water that isn't even that clean.

Every one of those businesses is a potential account. An office. A gym. A dental clinic. A car dealership. A warehouse. Each one is leaving money on the table — yours.

PureFlow reps don't cold-pitch. They walk in, offer a free cooler trial, let the team taste the difference, and ask one question: "Want to keep it?" The product sells itself. The free trial closes the deal.

And unlike most sales gigs where you get paid once and move on, PureFlow pays you every month for as long as that business keeps the system. Stack accounts over a year and the math gets real interesting, real fast.
