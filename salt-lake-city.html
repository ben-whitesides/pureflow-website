Salt Lake City Business Water & Ice

Bottleless water and ice coolers for Salt Lake City offices, gyms, clinics, and facilities. Installed and serviced by a local team — one flat monthly price, no bottles.

Salt Lake City's municipal water comes primarily from mountain snowmelt collected in Big Cottonwood, Little Cottonwood, Parleys, and City Creek canyons. While the treatment process meets federal standards, the water picks up dissolved minerals as it travels through aging infrastructure to your building. TDS readings across the Salt Lake Valley commonly range from 250 to 450 ppm.

For businesses, that means bottled-water delivery keeps piling up — jugs in the corner, rising invoices, and staff lifting 40-pound bottles. A bottleless PureFlow cooler connects to your existing water line and purifies on site, so your whole team gets clean water and ice without the hassle.

PureFlow serves businesses in Salt Lake City, Murray, Holladay, Millcreek, South Salt Lake, and surrounding communities. Every cooler is installed by a local technician and backed by all-inclusive maintenance.
