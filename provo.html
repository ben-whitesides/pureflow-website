Provo & Utah County Business Water & Ice

Bottleless water and ice coolers for Provo and Utah County businesses. Offices, gyms, clinics, and facilities — one flat monthly price, all maintenance included, no bottles.

Provo's water supply draws from a combination of Deer Creek Reservoir, the Provo River, and underground wells across Utah County. The treatment process meets EPA standards, but Utah County is known for having some of the hardest water along the entire Wasatch Front — businesses in Provo, Orem, Springville, and Spanish Fork regularly see TDS readings between 300 and 500 ppm.

Hard water is more than a nuisance for a business — it's a real cost. Scale buildup shortens the life of equipment and appliances, and bottled-water delivery only adds expense and clutter. A bottleless PureFlow cooler purifies on site and gives your whole team clean water and ice at one flat monthly price.

PureFlow serves businesses in Provo, Orem, Lindon, Pleasant Grove, Springville, Spanish Fork, and the surrounding Utah County communities. Installations are handled by Parker, our South Territory technician who knows Utah County's water conditions firsthand.
