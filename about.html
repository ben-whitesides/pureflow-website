We're a Utah-based team on a mission to give every business along the Wasatch Front unlimited, truly clean water and ice — without the bottles, the deliveries, or the runaround.

Why We Started PureFlow

We're local guys who got tired of watching Utah businesses overpay for water. Bottle delivery is expensive, unreliable, and wasteful — jugs stacking up in the corner, invoices creeping up every quarter, and someone on your team stuck lifting 40-pound bottles onto a cooler.

Then there's the water itself. The Wasatch Front has some of the hardest water in the country — TDS levels above 350 ppm, 89 contaminants detected across Utah's systems, and federal standards that haven't been updated in nearly 20 years. "Passing" doesn't mean clean.

We started PureFlow to fix both problems at once: purified, bottleless water and ice on tap for your whole team, at one flat monthly price with every service visit included. Installed and maintained by local technicians who actually pick up the phone.

Hard Water

The Wasatch Front has some of the hardest water in the western US. Calcium and magnesium cause scale buildup in equipment and appliances — shortening their lifespan and driving up energy costs for your facility.

🧪

High TDS Levels

Utah tap water regularly exceeds 350 ppm total dissolved solids — chlorine, fluoride, nitrates, and trace metals that affect taste, smell, and long-term health. Federal standards haven't been updated in nearly 20 years.
